# ADR 000: Decision title

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Owners: milestone or contributors
- Related requirements: IDs or specification sections
- Supersedes: none

## Context

Describe the decision that must be made, constraints, measured evidence, and why
the decision matters beyond one local implementation detail.

## Decision

State the selected approach precisely enough that another agent can implement or
review it without guessing.

## Alternatives considered

Describe credible alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Consequences

List positive, negative, operational, compatibility, privacy, safety, and future
migration effects.

## Validation

Define the evidence or tests that show the decision works as intended.

## Follow-up

List tasks, later decisions, or conditions that would justify revisiting this
ADR.
